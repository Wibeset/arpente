Export a walk or hike as GPX on iPhone

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Arpente records your walks and hikes with GPS, and every route can leave the app as a GPX file: the open format that most mapping, hiking and GPS apps can read.

What is a GPX file?

GPX (GPS Exchange Format) is a plain text file that lists the points of a route: their latitude, longitude, elevation and time. Because it’s an open standard, a GPX file recorded in one app can be opened in many others, on a phone, a computer or a GPS device.

Export a route from Arpente

  1. Record a walk or hike: tap record, walk, then tap Finish.
  2. Open the exploration in your journal.
  3. Tap Share, then Export as GPX.
  4. Save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it straight to another app.

What you can do with it

  • Keep an archive of your routes outside any app.
  • Open it in a mapping or hiking app that imports GPX.
  • Send it to a friend who wants to walk the same loop.
  • Look at it on a computer in any GPX viewer.

A word of caution

A GPX file contains the exact points of your route, including where it starts and ends. If a walk starts at your home, think twice before sharing its file publicly. Inside Arpente, your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud: see why Arpente is a private hiking tracker.
