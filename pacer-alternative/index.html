The best Pacer alternatives for walking

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Pacer counts your steps. If you’d rather remember your walks than count them, with a map of where you went and a few photos, here are the best alternatives, starting with ours.

The best Pacer alternatives

  1. 1. Arpente

    Best for: Remembering where you walked, not just how many steps

    A free walking journal for iPhone. Arpente draws each walk on a map with GPS and keeps its distance, time and elevation, with a note and photos. No goals to hit, no challenges, no account and no subscription: your walks stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

  2. 2. Map My Walk

    Best for: Walking for fitness, with goals and challenges

    A walking tracker from MapMyFitness, built around fitness: pace, calories, goals and challenges with friends. The free version shows ads; the MVP subscription removes them.

  3. 3. Walkmeter

    Best for: People who love detailed stats

    A detailed GPS tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch, with graphs, splits, announcements and GPX export. An Elite upgrade unlocks more features.

  4. 4. Strava

    Best for: Runners and cyclists who train with friends

    The social network of endurance sport. Strava records runs, rides, walks and hikes, with a feed, kudos, segments and leaderboards. Route building, offline routes and off-route alerts are part of the paid subscription.

  5. 5. AllTrails

    Best for: Finding a trail near you

    A huge library of trails, with reviews and photos from other hikers. Recording is free; offline maps and off-route alerts come with AllTrails Plus, and custom routes with Peak.

Why walkers and hikers choose Arpente

  • Walks, not steps. Each walk is kept with its route on a map, so you remember where you went, not just how far.
  • No pressure. No daily goal, no streak to keep, no challenge to win.
  • Keep the day. A name, a note and photos for each walk.
  • No account, no subscription. Arpente is free, with no ads and no paid tier.
  • Private. Your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

When Pacer may suit you better

If you want to count steps all day long without opening an app, follow guided workouts, or walk with groups and challenges, Pacer is made for that. Arpente records the walks you choose to keep, with their map.
