A free AllTrails alternative to record your hikes

Last updated: November 21, 2026

AllTrails helps you find a trail. Arpente keeps the ones you’ve walked: your route on a map, its distance, time and elevation, and a few words about the day. Free, with no account and no subscription.

What Arpente does

  • Record in one tap. Put your iPhone in your pocket: Arpente draws your route with GPS and measures the distance, time and elevation gain, even with the screen off.
  • Keep the day. Add a note and photos to each hike. Your journal fills up, month after month.
  • See where you’ve been. Every route on one map, and clear statistics by month and by year.
  • No account, no subscription. Everything is free, and nothing is locked behind a paid plan.
  • Private. Your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive them.

When AllTrails may suit you better

If you want to discover new trails, read other hikers’ reviews and photos, or follow a route someone else has drawn, AllTrails is made for that, and Arpente isn’t. Many hikers use both: AllTrails to pick the trail, Arpente to keep the hike.

Take your hikes anywhere

Each route can be exported as a GPX file, so your hikes are never locked inside Arpente.
