The best AllTrails alternatives to record your hikes

Last updated: November 23, 2026

AllTrails helps you find a trail. But once you’ve walked it, where does the hike live? Here are the best AllTrails alternatives, starting with the one made to keep your hikes.

The best AllTrails alternatives

  1. 1. Arpente

    Best for: Keeping every hike, for free, without an account

    A free hiking and walking journal for iPhone. Put your iPhone in your pocket: Arpente draws your route and measures the distance, time and elevation gain, even with the screen off. Add a note and photos, see every route on one map, and export any hike as GPX. Nothing is locked behind a paid plan.

  2. 2. Gaia GPS

    Best for: Backcountry navigation

    A navigation app for the backcountry, with many map layers: topographic, satellite, public lands and more. Offline maps and most layers need a Premium membership.

  3. 3. Wikiloc

    Best for: Trails recorded by others, all over the world

    Millions of trails shared by its community, in almost every country. Recording and following your own trails is free; navigating other people’s trails is part of Wikiloc Premium.

  4. 4. Komoot

    Best for: Planning routes, on foot or by bike

    A route planner for hiking and cycling, with turn-by-turn voice navigation and highlights shared by the community. Maps are sold by region or come with Komoot Premium.

  5. 5. Outdooractive

    Best for: Planning hikes in Europe

    Route planning, detailed maps and official content from tourism offices and parks, especially strong in the Alps and the rest of Europe. Offline and premium maps come with Pro and Pro+.

Why walkers and hikers choose Arpente

  • Record in one tap. Arpente draws your route with GPS and measures distance, time and elevation gain, even with the screen off.
  • Keep the day. Add a note and photos to each hike. Your journal fills up, month after month.
  • See where you’ve been. Every route on one map, and clear statistics by month and by year.
  • No account, no subscription. Everything is free, and nothing is locked behind a paid plan.
  • Private. Your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive them.

When AllTrails may suit you better

If you want to discover new trails, read other hikers’ reviews and photos, follow a route someone else has drawn, or download offline trail maps, AllTrails is made for that, and Arpente isn’t. Many hikers use both: AllTrails to pick the trail, Arpente to keep the hike.
