Questions, answered

Price

Is Arpente free?

Yes. Arpente is free, with no ads, no subscription and no account.

Account

Do I need an account?

No. Arpente works without an account or sign-up. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and synced with your private iCloud, which we can’t access.

The idea

Is Arpente a fitness app?

Not really. Arpente doesn’t rank you, compare your pace or push you to go faster. It keeps a journal of where you’ve been: a 2 km walk around the block is as worth keeping as an 18 km hike in the mountains.

Privacy

Who can see my routes?

Only you. There’s no public profile, no feed and no followers. Your routes show where you live and where you walk, so they stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud, and we never receive them.

Recording

How do I record an exploration?

Tap the record button and put your iPhone in your pocket. Arpente draws your route with GPS and measures the distance, time and elevation gain. Add a note or a photo along the way, then tap Finish when you’re back.

Backups

What if I change iPhones?

Your journal syncs with your iCloud and comes back on your new iPhone, with every route, note and photo.

GPX

Can I export my routes?

Yes. Each route can be exported as a GPX file, the open format that most mapping and hiking apps can read.

Android

Is Arpente available on Android?

No. Arpente is available for iPhone, in English and French.
