The best Map My Walk alternatives without ads

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Map My Walk treats walking as a workout: calories, pace, goals, with ads unless you pay. If you walk to see places, clear your head or explore your neighbourhood, here are the best alternatives, starting with ours.

The best Map My Walk alternatives

  1. 1. Arpente

    Best for: Walkers who want a journal with no ads and no subscription

    A free walking journal for iPhone. Arpente draws each walk on a map and keeps its distance, time and elevation, with a note and photos. No ads, no subscription, no account, and no calories to count: your walks stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

  2. 2. Pacer

    Best for: Counting your steps every day

    A pedometer and step counter with GPS walks, daily goals, groups and challenges. Free, with a paid Premium plan for guided workouts and more.

  3. 3. Strava

    Best for: Runners and cyclists who train with friends

    The social network of endurance sport. Strava records runs, rides, walks and hikes, with a feed, kudos, segments and leaderboards. Route building, offline routes and off-route alerts are part of the paid subscription.

  4. 4. Walkmeter

    Best for: People who love detailed stats

    A detailed GPS tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch, with graphs, splits, announcements and GPX export. An Elite upgrade unlocks more features.

  5. 5. AllTrails

    Best for: Finding a trail near you

    A huge library of trails, with reviews and photos from other hikers. Recording is free; offline maps and off-route alerts come with AllTrails Plus, and custom routes with Peak.

Why walkers and hikers choose Arpente

  • No ads, ever. Arpente has no ads and no paid tier to remove them.
  • No account. Open the app and tap record. No email, no password, no profile.
  • A journal, not a workout. Each walk keeps its map, its numbers, a note and the photos of the day.
  • Private. Your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Look back. Your journal month by month, statistics by month and by year, and a map of every place you’ve walked.

When Map My Walk may suit you better

If you walk to lose weight or train, want calorie and pace tracking, coaching, challenges with friends, or use an Android phone, Map My Walk does more on that side. Arpente is for keeping the story of your walks.
