Three walks in Montréal

Last updated: November 21, 2026

You don’t need a mountain range to have a story worth keeping. Three Montréal walks, from the water to the top of the mountain, and one that starts at your door.

The trail pages

  • Lachine Canal14.5 km one way, from the Old Port to Lachine
  • Mount RoyalAbout 1.4 km from the top of rue Peel to the lookout, about 2 km to the cross

The Lachine Canal

A flat path along the water, past old factories, locks and the Atwater Market. The full canal runs about 14.5 km from the Old Port to Lachine, but any stretch makes a good walk: start at the Old Port and turn back whenever you like.

Mount Royal, up to the Kondiaronk lookout

From the top of rue Peel, the trail climbs through the woods to Chemin Olmsted and the Kondiaronk lookout, with downtown at your feet: about 1.4 km one way. Keep going on the summit loop around the Chalet and the cross for a longer walk.

A loop around your own block

Rosemont, Villeray, Verdun or wherever you live: a 2 km loop on a weekday evening, with no destination at all. It won’t make a postcard, but in a year your journal will show every street you’ve walked.

Distances are approximate, from Parks Canada and the City of Montréal.
