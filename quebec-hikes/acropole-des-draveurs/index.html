Acropole des Draveurs, above the Hautes-Gorges

Last updated: November 21, 2026

A short, steep climb to bare summits that look straight down on the Malbaie River, deep in one of the deepest valleys east of the Rockies.

Distance
About 10.4 km return to the first summit, 11.2 km to the third
Elevation gain
About 800 m
Time
4 to 6 h
Difficulty
Difficult, with stone steps
Start
Trail parking in the valley, in the park
Season

Elevation profile

The trail

The trail leaves the valley floor and climbs almost without a break, through maples and then conifers, over roots and large stone steps. It’s short for its height: most of the 800 m come in a few kilometres.

Near the top, the forest opens onto bare rock and stunted trees. The first summit already gives the famous view: the Malbaie River winding at the bottom of the gorge, between walls hundreds of metres high. Two more summits follow along the ridge, the third rising to about 1,048 m.

Come back the same way, taking your time on the steps.

Good to know

  • Hard on the knees. The descent is as demanding as the climb. Poles help.
  • Colder at the top. The summits are exposed: bring a warm layer, even on a hot day in the valley.
  • Seasonal trail. The Acropole trail closes for part of the year. Check opening dates and conditions on sepaq.com.
  • Park access. An entry fee applies in Québec national parks.
