A walking journal for your iPhone

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Some people keep a notebook of their walks. Arpente keeps it for you: every walk and hike with its map, its numbers and a few words about the day, in one journal you can scroll back through for years.

What goes in your journal

  • The route. Drawn with GPS while you walk, on an Apple Maps map.
  • The numbers. Distance, time and elevation gain, without pace charts or targets.
  • The day. A title, a place, a note and photos, so you remember more than a line on a map.
  • The big picture. Kilometres explored and hours outside by month and by year, and a map of every place you’ve walked.

Every walk counts

A journal isn’t a scoreboard. A 2 km loop around the block on a Wednesday evening belongs next to an 18 km hike in the mountains: both are part of your life outside. Arpente never ranks them, and never pushes you to go further.

Start a walking journal in three steps

  1. Open Arpente and tap record. No account to create.
  2. Put your iPhone in your pocket and walk. Add a note or a photo along the way if you like.
  3. Tap Finish when you’re back. The walk lands in your journal with its map.

Yours, and only yours

Your journal is stored on your iPhone and synced with your private iCloud. There is no feed and no public profile, and we never receive your routes. Read the privacy policy.
