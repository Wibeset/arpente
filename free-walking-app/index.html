A free walking app for iPhone, really free

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Many “free” walking apps lock maps, statistics or exports behind a subscription, or pay for themselves with ads. Arpente is free, with no ads, no subscription and nothing held back.

Everything included, for free

  • GPS recording of your walks and hikes, even with the screen off.
  • Maps of every route, and one map of every place you’ve explored.
  • Distance, time and elevation for each walk, and statistics by month and by year.
  • Notes and photos to remember the day.
  • iCloud sync to find your journal on a new iPhone.
  • GPX export of any route.

No catch

No ads, because ads mean tracking. No subscription, because a journal shouldn’t expire. No account, because we don’t need to know who you are. Arpente is made by Wibeset, a small studio in Canada that also makes Reliure and Porcom.

For walking, not for racing

Arpente doesn’t count you against anyone. It keeps a walking journal of where you’ve been, from a lap of the park to a day in the mountains.

Coming soon

Arpente is coming soon to the App Store, free, for iPhone. Discover Arpente.
