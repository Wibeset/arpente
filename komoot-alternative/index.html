The best Komoot alternatives for walks and hikes

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Komoot plans your next tour. If all you want is to record a walk or a hike and keep it, without buying map regions or a premium plan, these are the best alternatives, starting with ours.

The best Komoot alternatives

  1. 1. Arpente

    Best for: Recording and keeping your walks, with nothing to buy

    A free journal of your walks and hikes for iPhone. Tap record, walk, tap Finish: your route, distance, time and elevation are saved with a note and photos. No map regions to unlock, no premium plan, no followers. Your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

  2. 2. Outdooractive

    Best for: Planning hikes in Europe

    Route planning, detailed maps and official content from tourism offices and parks, especially strong in the Alps and the rest of Europe. Offline and premium maps come with Pro and Pro+.

  3. 3. Wikiloc

    Best for: Trails recorded by others, all over the world

    Millions of trails shared by its community, in almost every country. Recording and following your own trails is free; navigating other people’s trails is part of Wikiloc Premium.

  4. 4. AllTrails

    Best for: Finding a trail near you

    A huge library of trails, with reviews and photos from other hikers. Recording is free; offline maps and off-route alerts come with AllTrails Plus, and custom routes with Peak.

  5. 5. Organic Maps

    Best for: Free offline maps

    A free, open-source offline map app based on OpenStreetMap, with hiking trails, contour lines and track recording. No account and no ads, but no journal either.

Why walkers and hikers choose Arpente

  • Nothing to plan. Tap record, walk, tap Finish. Your route, distance, time and elevation are saved.
  • Nothing to buy. No map regions to unlock and no premium plan.
  • Nothing to share. No profile, no followers. Your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.
  • Everything to keep. A note, photos, and a map of every place you’ve explored.

When Komoot may suit you better

If you plan routes ahead, need voice navigation on the trail, ride a bike as much as you walk, or want to share tours with friends, Komoot is the better tool. Arpente is for keeping what you’ve walked, not planning what comes next.
