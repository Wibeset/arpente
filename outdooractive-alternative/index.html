The best Outdooractive alternatives for walks and hikes

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Outdooractive is a complete planning platform for the outdoors. If you mostly want to record your walks and hikes and look back on them, a lighter app may suit you better. Here are the best alternatives, starting with ours.

The best Outdooractive alternatives

  1. 1. Arpente

    Best for: Recording and remembering your outings, without a Pro plan

    A free journal of your walks and hikes for iPhone. Arpente draws your route with GPS and keeps its distance, time and elevation gain, with a note and a few photos. Your journal, your statistics by month and by year, and a map of every place you’ve explored. No account, no Pro or Pro+, no ads.

  2. 2. Komoot

    Best for: Planning routes, on foot or by bike

    A route planner for hiking and cycling, with turn-by-turn voice navigation and highlights shared by the community. Maps are sold by region or come with Komoot Premium.

  3. 3. Wikiloc

    Best for: Trails recorded by others, all over the world

    Millions of trails shared by its community, in almost every country. Recording and following your own trails is free; navigating other people’s trails is part of Wikiloc Premium.

  4. 4. AllTrails

    Best for: Finding a trail near you

    A huge library of trails, with reviews and photos from other hikers. Recording is free; offline maps and off-route alerts come with AllTrails Plus, and custom routes with Peak.

  5. 5. Gaia GPS

    Best for: Backcountry navigation

    A navigation app for the backcountry, with many map layers: topographic, satellite, public lands and more. Offline maps and most layers need a Premium membership.

Why walkers and hikers choose Arpente

  • Light and quick. One tap to record, and your outing is saved with its map and numbers.
  • Nothing to subscribe to. No Pro, no Pro+, no ads: Arpente is free.
  • Private. No account and no profile. Your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.
  • Made to look back. A note and photos for each outing, a journal month by month, and clear statistics.
  • Your data, portable. Export any route as GPX and open it in Outdooractive or another app.

When Outdooractive may suit you better

If you plan multi-day tours, want official routes from tourism offices and parks, premium topographic maps of the Alps, or offline navigation, Outdooractive is the better tool. Arpente doesn’t plan routes: it keeps the ones you’ve walked.
