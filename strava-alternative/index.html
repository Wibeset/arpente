A Strava alternative, without the leaderboards

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Arpente is a free journal of your walks and hikes for iPhone. It draws your route, keeps your distance, time and elevation, and stops there: no feed, no followers, no segments to win.

Why walkers and hikers choose Arpente

  • No one to beat. Arpente doesn’t compare your pace or rank you on a segment. A slow loop with the dog counts as much as a summit.
  • Private by design. A route shows where you live and where you go. Arpente keeps yours on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, and we never receive them. There is no public profile to configure and no heatmap to opt out of.
  • No account. Open the app and tap record. No sign-up, no password, no email.
  • A journal, not a log. Each exploration keeps its map, its numbers, a note and the photos of the day.
  • Your routes stay yours. Export any route as a GPX file, the open format most mapping apps can read.

When Strava may suit you better

Strava is built for training and community. If you run or ride, want to follow friends, chase segments, analyse heart rate and power, or use Android or a web browser, Strava does far more than Arpente. Arpente does one thing: keep the story of where you’ve walked.

Keep both, if you like

Nothing stops you from keeping Strava for your runs and Arpente for your walks and hikes. Your Arpente journal never leaves your iPhone and your iCloud, so the two never mix.
