The best Wikiloc alternatives for walks and hikes

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Wikiloc is a giant public library of trails. But not every walk is meant to be published. If you’d rather keep your routes to yourself, here are the best Wikiloc alternatives, starting with ours.

The best Wikiloc alternatives

  1. 1. Arpente

    Best for: Keeping your routes private instead of publishing them

    A free journal of your walks and hikes for iPhone. Arpente records your route with GPS, keeps its distance, time and elevation, and lets you add a note and photos. Nothing is published, nothing needs an account: your routes stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. No ads, no Premium.

  2. 2. AllTrails

    Best for: Finding a trail near you

    A huge library of trails, with reviews and photos from other hikers. Recording is free; offline maps and off-route alerts come with AllTrails Plus, and custom routes with Peak.

  3. 3. Outdooractive

    Best for: Planning hikes in Europe

    Route planning, detailed maps and official content from tourism offices and parks, especially strong in the Alps and the rest of Europe. Offline and premium maps come with Pro and Pro+.

  4. 4. Komoot

    Best for: Planning routes, on foot or by bike

    A route planner for hiking and cycling, with turn-by-turn voice navigation and highlights shared by the community. Maps are sold by region or come with Komoot Premium.

  5. 5. Organic Maps

    Best for: Free offline maps

    A free, open-source offline map app based on OpenStreetMap, with hiking trails, contour lines and track recording. No account and no ads, but no journal either.

Why walkers and hikers choose Arpente

  • Private, not public. Your routes are never uploaded to a shared library. They stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.
  • No account, no ads. Open the app and record. No sign-up and no banners.
  • Everything included. There is no Premium tier: every feature is free.
  • A journal, not a catalogue. A note and photos for each walk, your statistics by month and by year, and a map of every place you’ve explored.
  • GPX in one tap. Export any route as GPX and open it in Wikiloc or another app if you ever want to share it.

When Wikiloc may suit you better

If you’re looking for trails in a new region, want to follow a route recorded by someone else, or like to share your own trails with a community, Wikiloc’s library is hard to beat. Arpente doesn’t find trails: it keeps the ones you’ve walked.
